Help

  • Click any item, slot, or augment color to collapse or expand it - tuck away colors you don't have or effects you've already decided on.
  • Save to Cloud keeps your build online. Open loads one you saved before. Download makes a shareable report.
  • Named Items are new and still a work in progress - for now, picking the closest existing match by name is the best this app can do. Use the Description field to jot down what's actually different about your item, so you (or a future you) can sort it out later.
